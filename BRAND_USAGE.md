# CeloHT Brand Usage Guide

The design files in this repository are licensed under CC BY 4.0 (see LICENSE) — you may reuse and adapt them with attribution.

The **CeloHT name and logo as an identifier of the CeloHT organization** are treated separately from that copyright license, the same way a company's open-source code being MIT-licensed doesn't give you the right to put their logo on your own product as if you were them.

## You may, with attribution
- Reference the CeloHT logo in press coverage, academic research, or a partner-organization page describing a real collaboration with CeloHT
- Use brand colors/typography guidance as a starting point for your own unrelated project, once clearly separated from the CeloHT identity

## Please ask first (contact@celoht.com)
- Using the CeloHT name or logo in a way that could suggest your product, token, or service is made, endorsed, or approved by CeloHT
- Modifying the logo itself and re-publishing the modified version as "the CeloHT logo"
- Any commercial merchandise using the CeloHT mark

## Always prohibited
- Using the CeloHT name or logo in connection with any token, coin, presale, or investment offer — see the org-wide [No-Token Policy](https://github.com/Celo-HaiTi/CeloHT/blob/main/NO_TOKEN_POLICY.md). Report misuse to security@celoht.com.
